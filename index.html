Live K–12 classes in a 3D world

Students learn K–12 subjects in small, live online classes—together in the Physics Playground, an interactive 3D classroom you can join from home.

Enrollment, scheduling, payment, and class time all happen on Outschool. During the session, learners also join a shared online room in the Physics Playground so they can explore the lesson side by side.
